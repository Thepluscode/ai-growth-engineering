# Outreach Research

Build one account-level message from:
- ICP fit
- observable commercial signal
- plausible problem
- evidence confidence
- one low-friction CTA

Quality score:
- ICP fit 0–5
- decision-maker fit 0–5
- research evidence 0–5
- pain relevance 0–5
- message specificity 0–5
- CTA friction 0–5

<18: do not send
18–23: review
24+: send eligible

Never send to a suppressed identity.
