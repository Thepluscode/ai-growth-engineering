# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-08-19*

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** The Digital Marketing Project builds and operates measurable systems that acquire
customers, increase conversion and grow revenue; AI Growth Engineering is its evidence-driven,
AI-enabled operating methodology.

**What it does:** The project spans market intelligence, strategy, creative, distribution,
conversion, revenue operations and measurement. Work is organised into bounded experiments that
connect evidence to a hypothesis, campaign, commercial result and learning. Software and automation
are implementation tools, built only when repeated marketing workflows justify them.

**Product category:** Digital marketing services and growth experimentation.

**Product type:** Service-led operating business with an internal engineering and automation layer;
not currently a customer-facing SaaS product.

**Business model:** Current commercial ladder is a free Growth Leak Teardown, a £1,000–£2,500 Growth
System Diagnostic, a £3,000–£7,500 30-Day Pipeline Engineering Sprint, and a potential
£2,000–£8,000+ monthly Growth Engineering Retainer. Pricing remains a hypothesis until customers pay.

## Target Audience

**Target companies:** Markets are unrestricted and validated one experiment at a time. The active
experiment, `EXP-ACQ-0001`, targets UK cybersecurity consultancies and managed-service providers.

**Decision-makers:** Defined per experiment. For `EXP-ACQ-0001`: founders, managing directors,
commercial leaders and marketing leaders.

**Primary use case:** Find and improve the largest measurable leak between market attention,
qualified pipeline and revenue.

**Jobs to be done:**

- Diagnose why current marketing activity is not becoming qualified commercial conversations.
- Design and run a bounded marketing experiment with a decision threshold.
- Connect channel, conversion and sales evidence to customer-acquisition economics.

**Use cases:**

- Market, competitor and customer research
- Positioning, offer and messaging experiments
- Creative and distribution experiments across organic, paid, outbound and partner channels
- Landing-page, lead-capture and qualification improvement
- Attribution, CAC, LTV, contribution-profit, pipeline and revenue measurement

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**

- Observed facts are separated from inference.
- Experiments are preregistered with metrics, thresholds and minimum samples.
- Work is evaluated against qualified pipeline, revenue and unit economics rather than vanity metrics.
- AI and automation accelerate controlled workflows but do not replace evidence or authority.

**How we do it differently:** Evidence → hypothesis → campaign → experiment → conversion → revenue →
learning.

**Why that's better:** It makes marketing decisions falsifiable and preserves the evidence behind
commercial claims.

**Why customers choose us:** Not yet known; requires paying-customer evidence.
