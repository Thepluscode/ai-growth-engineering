An AI control can only act on the business state it can see.

We tested that boundary with one pinned model, a refund sandbox and scenario-specific business rules.

In BLOCK-002, all 30 uncontrolled runs issued a refund that breached a 24-hour cumulative limit. When the control could see the prior refund history, all 30 governed live runs prevented that effect.

Then LIMIT-001 exposed the opposite result: 29 of 30 governed live runs still produced an unsafe refund because the fraud-hold fact never reached the control.

The point is not that “governance works” or “governance fails.”

It is narrower: identity and policy do not substitute for the business context required to make the decision.

We published the run-level evidence, exact-action replays, verification manifest and limits here:
https://theplus-tech.com/research/agent-under-oath

If you operate AI agents in a consequential workflow: what business fact should make the decision flip—and can your control actually see it?
