# First 30 Days

## Immediate milestone

- 20 qualified prospect records
- 10 deep researches
- 10 evidence-backed teardown decisions
- 10 qualified manual sends
- First meaningful replies
- First discovery call

Foresite is retained as a researched disqualification and replaced in the send batch by Morcan IT
Solutions. Do not count a staged draft as a send.

## Gate

- 100 qualified prospects
- 100 sends
- 20 meaningful responses
- 10 discovery calls
- 5 diagnostics proposed
- 3 proposals
- 1 paying customer
- £5k stretch collected revenue

## Week 1 — Build the learning loop

- Seed first 20 prospects.
- Deep-research first 10.
- Create one teardown per researched account.
- Preregister `EXP-ACQ-0001`.
- Send first 10 messages only after quality review.

## Week 2 — Customer language

- Record objections and exact phrases.
- Conduct discovery calls.
- Add demand evidence.
- Do not change the core hypothesis until minimum evidence is reached unless the experiment is invalid.

## Week 3 — Sell the Sprint

- Convert strongest repeated pain into diagnostic scope.
- Propose £3k–£7.5k Sprint where economics justify it.
- Preregister the client experiment before implementation.

## Week 4 — Evidence

- Produce the evidence report.
- Record what moved, what did not, why, cost and commercial consequence.
- Convert result into first case study only if claims are supportable.

## Kill logic

For `EXP-ACQ-0001`:

- >=10% meaningful reply after 50 qualified sends: KEEP
- 5–9.9%: ITERATE
- <5%: KILL / materially revise
- <50 sends: INVALID for final decision
