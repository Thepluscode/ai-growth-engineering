# `EXP-ACQ-0001` Commercial Offer

This is the service ladder under test for the current UK Cyber/MSP acquisition experiment. It is not
the permanent offer or full scope of the Digital Marketing Project.

## Entry: Growth Leak Teardown

One page:

1. observable signal
2. likely economic leak
3. fact vs inference
4. smallest valid experiment
5. primary metric
6. low-friction CTA

## Core: 30-Day Pipeline Engineering Sprint

Promise:

> Find the largest measurable leak between market attention and qualified pipeline, implement one bounded intervention, and produce evidence showing whether the economics improved.

### Components

- Growth System Diagnostic
- Growth Leak Map
- Customer Evidence Pack
- One engineered intervention
- Preregistered experiment
- Day-30 evidence report

### Price hypothesis

£3,000–£7,500 + media spend where applicable.

### Boundaries

Do not promise revenue growth that depends on factors outside the engagement's control.
Do not sell vanity metrics.
Do not claim compliance, profitability or customer results without evidence.
