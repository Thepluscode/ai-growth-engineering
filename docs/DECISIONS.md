# Decision Log

## D-001 — Service before SaaS

**Decision:** Do not build a customer-facing Growth SaaS before repeated paid workflow evidence.

**Reason:** production capability is not the bottleneck; market proof is.

## D-002 — UK cyber/MSP wedge

**Decision:** start with UK cybersecurity consultancies and MSPs.

**Reason:** high contract values, familiar buyer language, and measurable B2B pipeline economics.

## D-003 — Rule of One

**Decision:** during validation keep one buyer, one problem, one offer, one CTA, one primary channel and one primary metric.

## D-004 — Outbound before content scale

**Decision:** direct B2B outbound is the primary acquisition engine; evidence-led content is secondary until the first customer exists.

## D-005 — No bulk autonomous outreach

**Decision:** bulk outreach is an approval-required material action; suppressed identities are hard-denied.

## D-006 — Revenue claims require lineage

**Decision:** no public performance claim without source evidence and review.

## D-007 — Competitors are references, not templates

**Decision:** extract functions/patterns; do not clone competitor copy, design, testimonials or brand assets.
