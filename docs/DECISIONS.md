# Decision Log

## D-000 — Digital Marketing is the project; AI Growth Engineering is the method

**Decision:** operate this repository as the engineering and automation layer of the Digital
Marketing Project. Markets and channels are unrestricted but tested through bounded, preregistered
experiments.

**Reason:** market research, strategy, creative, distribution, conversion, revenue and measurement
are the operating domain. AI, automation, evidence controls and experiment tooling support that work;
they are not a separate market or a cybersecurity product.

## D-001 — Service before SaaS

**Decision:** Do not build a customer-facing Growth SaaS before repeated paid workflow evidence.

**Reason:** production capability is not the bottleneck; market proof is.

## D-002 — UK cyber/MSP wedge

**Decision:** use UK cybersecurity consultancies and MSPs for `EXP-ACQ-0001`.

**Reason:** high contract values, familiar buyer language, and measurable B2B pipeline economics make
this a suitable first market test. The decision does not constrain later Digital Marketing experiments.

## D-003 — Rule of One

**Decision:** within each validation experiment keep one buyer, one problem, one offer, one CTA, one
primary channel and one primary metric.

## D-004 — Outbound before content scale

**Decision:** direct B2B outbound is the primary acquisition channel for `EXP-ACQ-0001`; evidence-led
content is secondary within that experiment until the first customer exists.

## D-005 — No bulk autonomous outreach

**Decision:** bulk outreach is an approval-required material action; suppressed identities are hard-denied.

## D-006 — Revenue claims require lineage

**Decision:** no public performance claim without source evidence and review.

## D-007 — Competitors are references, not templates

**Decision:** extract functions/patterns; do not clone competitor copy, design, testimonials or brand assets.
