# Growth Leak Teardown — Morcan IT Solutions

Research date: 2026-08-19

Replacement note: promoted from seeded priority B because Foresite failed the UK-wedge qualification gate.

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** London security-first MSP serving UK SMEs, led by Managing Director Roger Eales.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Managed IT, cloud migration, Microsoft 365, continuous security posture, cyber services, open-source/private-cloud options, AI consultancy, and personal senior-engineer support.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** The homepage presents “Book a Free Migration Assessment,” “Get a Free Posture Assessment,” “Book a Free Security Review,” and service-specific assessment routes.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Detailed homepage; service pages; assessment CTAs; operational/service metrics; testimonials; phone and enquiry email; specific cloud/security solution pages.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Multiple service/problem sections → one of several free assessment/review CTAs → booking/contact route.
- **Unknown:** Whether CTAs share one flow, visitor comprehension, conversion by intent, qualification, CAC, and close rate.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Named Managing Director; customer testimonials; Microsoft/NCSC positioning; stated service metrics; detailed assessment scope; explicit technical architecture on service pages.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Several overlapping labels—migration assessment, posture assessment, security review, and security assessment—may fragment measurement or make the intended first step unclear for buyers whose need spans cloud and security.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Netitude:** centres one named Net9 audit and defines its evidence, score, report, and roadmap ([source](https://www.netitude.co.uk/net9)).
2. **The HBP Group:** uses one 360° Assessment concept across IT risks and bottlenecks ([source](https://thehbpgroup.co.uk/)).
3. **Secure Chain:** repeats one posture-assessment CTA and one short trigger-focused form ([source](https://securechaingroup.com/)).

## One hypothesis

One umbrella “Security & Cloud Posture Review,” followed by intent routing, will produce more qualified bookings and cleaner attribution than multiple overlapping free-assessment labels.

## One proposed experiment

On the homepage only, test a single umbrella CTA that opens a two-choice route—security posture or cloud migration—while keeping the existing underlying forms. Compare qualified bookings and correctly routed conversations with the current multi-label version.
