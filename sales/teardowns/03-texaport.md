# Growth Leak Teardown — Texaport

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK strategic IT, managed-services, and cybersecurity provider for growing businesses, with UK regional pages and a named Founder/CEO, Hugh Caldwell.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit for the wedge.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Managed IT, global support, cloud, Microsoft 365, consultancy, digital transformation, cybersecurity consultancy, vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, training, Cyber Essentials, GDPR, and AI services.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** Homepage “Get in touch” and “Tell us what you need”; contact page asks visitors to send a message. Service pages also route toward contact/expert conversations.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Broad service architecture; industry pages; regional pages; case studies; guides/blog; Trustpilot; contact page; phone and regional email addresses.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Search/industry/region/service page → generic contact CTA → message form or phone/email.
- **Unknown:** Which surfaces produce opportunities, CTA conversion, lead qualification, CAC, sales cycle, and close rate.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Named case studies across IT and cyber, Microsoft partnership and Cyber Essentials Plus marks, a founder-backed personal guarantee, and customer statements tied to specific infrastructure/security outcomes.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Strong cyber and industry proof may lose commercial force when many high-intent routes converge on “Get in touch” or “Tell us what you need,” leaving the visitor to define the first engagement.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Netitude:** makes a report-producing audit the first step and explains each audit stage ([source](https://www.netitude.co.uk/it-audit)).
2. **Secure Chain:** pairs a specific posture-assessment CTA with sector and trigger qualification ([source](https://securechaingroup.com/)).
3. **Morcan:** specifies the exact systems reviewed in a free security review ([source](https://www.morcan.co.uk/)).

## One hypothesis

A bounded readiness assessment tied to one buyer trigger will convert more qualified cyber demand than a generic contact invitation on the Cyber Essentials and vulnerability-assessment routes.

## One proposed experiment

Test “Get your Cyber Essentials readiness map” on only the Cyber Essentials page. Offer a short scope review and written gap summary, place the most relevant named cyber case study beside it, and measure qualified calls per page visitor against the existing contact CTA.
