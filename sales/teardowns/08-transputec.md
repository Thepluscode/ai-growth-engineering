# Growth Leak Teardown — Transputec

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK managed IT, cybersecurity, cloud, modern workplace, consultancy, and AI provider serving mid-market and international organisations. The site names CEO Sonny Sehgal and a senior operating team.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit, though larger and more enterprise-facing than several queue accounts.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Six service pillars under one accountable team; managed IT is sold with 24/7 support, fixed per-seat pricing, a 12-month rolling term, service commitments, and an initial IT assessment.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** Homepage and service pages use service-specific routes such as “Get a strategic consultation.”

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Outcome-led homepage; six service pillars; service pages; problem-led sections; case studies; live-looking operational metrics; comparison tables; current insights; phone/contact routes.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Problem or service page → strategic-consultation CTA → contact route. Managed-IT pages include detailed commercial/proof information before the CTA.
- **Unknown:** Traffic mix, form completion, sales acceptance, opportunity creation, CAC, and close rate.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Named customer stories including World Rugby and Amber Infrastructure; operational metrics and certifications; average client tenure; fixed-fee/service details; named leadership.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** No obvious public-funnel defect is visible. The weaker possibility is that “strategic consultation” remains a broad ask for visitors in a specific switching event, despite strong transition proof and fixed-fee economics that could support a more concrete first step.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Netitude:** makes an audit with a written report the explicit first step ([source](https://www.netitude.co.uk/it-audit)).
2. **Morcan:** offers a migration assessment and a separate security review with named scope ([source](https://www.morcan.co.uk/)).
3. **The HBP Group:** names a 360° Assessment designed to expose risks and bottlenecks ([source](https://thehbpgroup.co.uk/)).

## One hypothesis

For visitors actively switching MSP, a transition-risk and cost-map CTA will produce more qualified conversations than a general strategic consultation.

## One proposed experiment

On the managed-IT page only, test “Map your MSP transition risk and 12-month cost” against “Get a strategic consultation.” Reuse the zero-disruption transition proof and fixed-fee details; deliver a scoped comparison, not a savings promise. Measure sales-accepted switching conversations per visitor.
