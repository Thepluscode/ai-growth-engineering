# Growth Leak Teardown — Wavex Technology

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** London MSP serving small to mid-market organisations across managed IT, cybersecurity, compliance, cloud, and AI. Gavin Russell is named CEO and Founder.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Proactive managed IT and continuous cybersecurity built around the proprietary APEX data platform, AI-augmented operations, CIS-aligned risk management, live reporting, and consulting/projects.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Speak to an expert” and “Book My Free Consultation” on current pages; contact can also be made by phone and general-enquiries email.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Homepage; managed-IT and cybersecurity pages; sector-specific pages; case studies; about/differentiation pages; educational resources; contact page.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Problem/service/case-study page → expert/free-consultation CTA → form asking company, employee count, desired team, and main challenge.
- **Unknown:** CTA conversion, qualification, meeting held rate, opportunity creation, CAC, and close rate.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Named leadership and long operating history; ISO 27001 and Microsoft credentials; case studies with explicit outputs; named client statements; APEX/CIS methodology and operational reporting are described in detail.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Wavex has unusually concrete differentiation, but the first step is still framed as a general “free consultation.” Buyers may not know what useful output they receive before agreeing to a sales conversation.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Netitude:** promises an audit report, scores, findings, and roadmap as the first step ([source](https://www.netitude.co.uk/net9)).
2. **The HBP Group:** names a 360° Assessment and its executive-level output ([source](https://thehbpgroup.co.uk/)).
3. **Secure Chain:** offers a posture assessment and asks the buyer’s trigger in a short form ([source](https://securechaingroup.com/)).

## One hypothesis

Giving the initial consultation a tangible CIS/APEX output will increase qualified bookings from security and compliance pages.

## One proposed experiment

On the cybersecurity page, test “Get a 20-minute CIS Risk Snapshot” against “Speak to an expert.” Use existing public inputs only, return a bounded risk-conversation outline rather than a security claim, and measure sales-accepted calls per visitor.
