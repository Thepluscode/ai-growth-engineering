# Growth Leak Teardown — Secure Chain

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK-based MSSP for SMEs and regulated firms, headquartered in Chesterfield. Current pages focus on managed security, VMaaS, testing, compliance, and UK delivery. Tim Brennan is named CEO/Director on a company page and linked to the company on LinkedIn.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit, subject to final role verification before sending.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Enterprise-grade security adapted for SMEs; managed detection, continuous vulnerability management, penetration testing, compliance support, sector routes, transparent delivery, and a complimentary posture assessment.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Book a posture assessment” and “Book a Consultation”; the form asks name, work email, company, sector, and what prompted the conversation.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Homepage; service and VMaaS pages; legal/healthcare/financial-services pages; pricing navigation; resources; case studies; trust centre; about pages; direct phone/email.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Sector/service/proof content → posture-assessment CTA → short qualification form.
- **Observed:** A separate indexed `/about-us/` page remains live with older styling/content and a placeholder `info@website.com`, while the current `/about` page and footer use the real company contact.
- **Unknown:** Traffic reaching the stale URL, exit rate, enquiry loss, and downstream conversion.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Current site names frameworks, Cyber Essentials Plus, UK SOC, client-portal transparency, and selected engagement outcomes. Some case studies are anonymised or available under NDA. A named Cyber Essentials testimonial appears on the older page.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The stale indexed `/about-us/` route may create avoidable trust loss at due-diligence stage because it conflicts with the current brand experience and exposes a placeholder email. This is unusually consequential for a security provider.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Littlefish:** consolidates accreditations, quantified SOC activity, operating model, and services on one current cyber page ([source](https://www.littlefish.co.uk/cyber-services/)).
2. **CloudTech24:** uses a current team page with named commercial and technical leadership ([source](https://cloudtech24.com/meet-the-team/)).
3. **Morcan:** combines named leadership, concrete assessment scope, and direct current contact details on one main route ([source](https://www.morcan.co.uk/)).

## One hypothesis

Consolidating the two about routes will reduce trust-stage exits and prevent lost enquiries from the placeholder contact route.

## One proposed experiment

First measure visits, exits, and enquiries attributable to `/about-us/`. Then 301-redirect it to `/about`, preserve any unique testimonial content on the current site, update the sitemap, and compare due-diligence-page exits and qualified enquiries for a fixed period. Do not claim uplift without enough traffic.
