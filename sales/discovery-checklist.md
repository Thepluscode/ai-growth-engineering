# Discovery Checklist

Record whether every number is measured, estimated, or unknown. Preserve the buyer's exact language.

## Required economics and funnel

- Typical customer value
- Gross margin
- Lead sources
- Monthly lead volume
- Qualified lead percentage
- Booking percentage
- Close percentage
- Current CAC
- Sales cycle
- Marketing spend
- Biggest perceived growth constraint

## Opportunity reconstruction

> Walk me through the last 10 opportunities you actually wanted.

For each opportunity, capture source, fit, stage reached, time in stage, outcome, loss reason, value,
and what evidence exists in the CRM.

## Commercial routing

- Strong economics, clear leak, accessible data: propose the 30-Day Pipeline Engineering Sprint (£3k-£7.5k).
- Hesitant buyer or unreliable data: propose the Growth System Diagnostic (£1k-£2.5k).
- No costly repeated problem, no buyer, or weak value-to-cost ratio: do not sell; record the disconfirming evidence.
