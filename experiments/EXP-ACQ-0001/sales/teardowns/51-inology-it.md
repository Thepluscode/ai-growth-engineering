# Growth Leak Teardown — Inology IT

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** Manchester MSP serving UK SMEs with managed IT, cyber-resilience and compliance support.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** An anonymous two-minute Cyber Score returns a 0–100 score, three gaps and board-ready summary; a separate Fit Finder recommends service tier and indicative price.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Get your score” and “Run the Fit Finder.”

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Homepage tools, managed-service tiers and pricing, security content, downloadable policies/checklists, email and phone.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** IT/cyber content → anonymous Cyber Score or Fit Finder → result → optional human contact.
- **Unknown:** Score-to-conversation rate, result persistence and whether high-risk results reach the commercial team with consent.

## Proof
- **Observed:** Transparent indicative pricing, defined service tiers, current resources, explicit SME fit and public contact routes.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Removing the email gate reduces friction, but the public journey may lack a sufficiently specific bridge from a salient score to a relevant expert conversation.

## Competitor patterns
1. **CyberLab:** includes expert review within its assessment process ([source](https://cyberlab.co.uk/services/posture-assessment/)).
2. **OryxAlign:** lets users email the score and improvement advice to themselves ([source](https://info.oryxalign.com/cyber-security-score)).
3. **Atlas Cloud:** combines a report with a short expert-results appointment ([source](https://atlascloud.co.uk/it-services/cyber-security-consultancy/mot/)).

## One hypothesis
A contextual, voluntary “review my three gaps” CTA will convert more qualified conversations without sacrificing anonymous completion.

## One proposed experiment
After score display, test a consent-based 15-minute review CTA preloaded with score band and top gap. Measure booked reviews and sales acceptance while keeping results ungated.
