# Growth Leak Teardown — ADL Consulting

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK boutique information-security consultancy focused on ISO 27001, GDPR, training, internal audit, fractional DPO and fractional CISO. Andy Larkum is named Managing Director.
- **Inferred:** Good fit for the cybersecurity-consultancy side of the wedge; narrower and more specialised than an MSP.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Custom-fit ISO 27001 ISMS implementation, outsourced internal audits, GDPR services, training, and fractional leadership. The offer emphasises low-maintenance systems, technical/auditing experience, and plain language.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Get in touch” is repeated across the homepage, ISO consultancy, internal-audit, and location pages; contact details are also provided.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Homepage; service pages; regional ISO pages; educational posts; testimonials/case-result posts; email and phone.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Search or educational/service page → “Get in touch” → general conversation.
- **Unknown:** Traffic, intent mix, enquiry qualification, sales-cycle stage, conversion, CAC, and close rate.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Named client testimonials; certification timelines/results in client stories; ADL’s own ISO 27001 certification; named technical/auditing methodology and long operating history.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The offer is specific, but the same generic CTA serves buyers at very different stages: early ISO exploration, urgent internal audit, surveillance preparation, and full implementation. The site may be under-capturing high-intent, time-bound audit demand.

## Competitor patterns

1. **IT Governance:** packages a pre-project gap analysis with named outputs and offers a fixed-scope FastTrack route ([source](https://www.itgovernance.co.uk/download/ISO27001ISM-Brochure_v1.2.pdf)).
2. **High Table:** publishes an explicit gap-analysis price and offers a downloadable demo without email capture ([source](https://hightable.io/iso-27001-gap-analysis-guide/), [demo](https://hightable.io/iso-27001-toolkit-demo/)).
3. **Cyber Tec Security:** separates solo, guided, managed, and pre-assessment routes with transparent starting prices ([source](https://www.cybertecsecurity.com/compare-pricing/)).

## One hypothesis

A stage-specific “ISO 27001 readiness call” that identifies audit date and current stage will create more qualified conversations than the general “Get in touch” CTA on ISO implementation pages.

## One proposed experiment

Test a short ISO readiness form on the main ISO 27001 consultancy page: target certification date, company size, current documentation status, internal owner, and need (implementation/internal audit/maintenance). Promise only a fit-and-scope response. Measure sales-accepted conversations per visitor against the existing CTA.
