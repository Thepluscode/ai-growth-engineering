# Growth Leak Teardown — M247

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** UK connectivity, cloud and managed-cyber provider serving growing organisations.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** A free M365 Security Assessment returns a posture score, peer benchmark and recommended measures; managed cyber adds health checks, hardening, XDR and 24/7 response.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Book for your free 365 security assessment” and “Get in touch.”

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Managed-security pages, M365 campaign, Digital Advisory, brochures, webinars, phone, sales email and forms.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** M365/cyber content → free assessment or generic contact → report/consultation → managed service.
- **Unknown:** Whether the assessment is visible from all high-intent cyber pages and assessment-to-managed-service conversion.

## Proof
- **Observed:** Defined assessment method, 24/7 response, named technology coverage and public sales/support channels.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The strongest bounded entry offer sits on a separate campaign URL while the broader managed-cyber page ends in a generic contact route.

## Competitor patterns
1. **Xperience:** repeats its free assessment directly within the main cyber overview ([source](https://www.xperience-group.com/solutions/cyber-security/cyber-security-overview/)).
2. **CyberLab:** embeds its assessment across consultancy and network-service journeys ([source](https://cyberlab.co.uk/services/consultancy/)).
3. **BCN:** connects MDR service content directly to its assessment ([source](https://bcn.co.uk/cyber-security/managed-detection-response/)).

## One hypothesis
Making the free M365 assessment the primary first step on M365-relevant managed-cyber pages will increase qualified assessment starts.

## One proposed experiment
On the managed-cyber page, test an assessment-led CTA with the score/report outputs against generic “Get in touch.” Measure completed assessments and sales-accepted conversations.
