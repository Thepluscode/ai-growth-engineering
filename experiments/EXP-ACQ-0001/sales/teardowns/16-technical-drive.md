# Growth Leak Teardown — Technical Drive

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** West Midlands MSP serving SMEs across IT support, cyber security, communications and digital services.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Managed IT and cyber services, including CyberSafe365, Cyber Essentials, SOC and a free Cyber Security Audit after a two-minute confidence calculator.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Speak to an IT Specialist”/contact route; the calculator page invites a score and then an audit.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Service and sector pages, calculator, articles, case studies, Google-review proof, phone with sales option and enquiry form.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Calculator → personalised score → free cyber audit → general form/contact route. The calculator article also contains an unrelated SharePoint-meeting prompt.
- **Unknown:** Score completion, score-to-audit conversion, context passed to sales, qualification and close rate.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Published support metrics, customer reviews, accreditations, named cases and a current service guide naming Managing Director Simon Cole.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The strong score-to-audit journey may lose intent and useful score context when it rejoins a generic form surrounded by unrelated page prompts.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Intergence:** promises a custom report after its assessment consultation ([source](https://www.intergence.com/event/cybersecurity-assessment-and-infrastructure-audit)).
2. **Slink:** ties its review to visible gaps, priorities and a practical plan ([source](https://slink.co.uk/)).
3. **Fortitude Cyber:** provides an explicit low-cost next step with scope and delivery time ([source](https://fortitudecyber.co.uk/cyber-grc/)).

## One hypothesis

A dedicated “Review my score” booking that carries calculator results into the conversation will produce more qualified cyber-audit bookings than the generic post-score contact route.

## One proposed experiment

For completed calculator users only, test a score-aware booking CTA and remove unrelated prompts from that state. Compare qualified audit bookings and sales acceptance with the current handoff.
