# Growth Leak Teardown — Waterstons

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** UK technology consultancy with dedicated cyber, managed-security, assessment and incident-response teams.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** SOC services, M365 Security Review, vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, framework work and incident response.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Contact us,” specialist email addresses and named-expert contact blocks.

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Service pages, specialist articles, named practitioners, PDFs, framework content, email, phone and contact pages.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** Specialist service/framework content → named expert or generic contact → scoped conversation.
- **Unknown:** Whether the buyer is routed to assessment, SOC or advisory based on maturity and whether source is retained.

## Proof
- **Observed:** Almost three decades' delivery, named technical leaders, CREST SOC evidence and detailed assessment methods.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The depth of specialist options may require buyers to choose the right technical starting point before Waterstons has assessed their maturity.

## Competitor patterns
1. **CyberLab:** offers a broad NCSC-aligned posture assessment as a first step ([source](https://cyberlab.co.uk/services/posture-assessment/)).
2. **Central Technology:** packages infrastructure, people and policy review into one starting product ([source](https://www.ct.co.uk/services/cyber-security-review/)).
3. **Atlas Cloud:** makes depth progression explicit from MOT to penetration testing ([source](https://atlascloud.co.uk/it-services/cyber-security-consultancy/)).

## One hypothesis
A short “help me choose the right security assessment” route will improve correct routing without diluting specialist service pages.

## One proposed experiment
On SOC and technical-assessment pages, test one maturity-routing CTA asking environment, trigger and desired assurance level. Compare first-touch routing and accepted opportunities.
