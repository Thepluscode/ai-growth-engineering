# Growth Leak Teardown — KHIPU Networks

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** UK cyber and network provider offering sector-specific SOC, awareness, assessment and infrastructure services.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** A 24/7 Community SOC designed for education and a security-awareness service that can include endpoint vulnerability assessment and simulated attacks.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** General sales email and phone routes in service collateral.

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Sector solution briefs, service PDFs, customer case studies, training content, phone and sales email.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** Sector/service collateral → general sales contact → scoped conversation.
- **Unknown:** Whether sector, service and content asset are retained and their accepted-opportunity rates.

## Proof
- **Observed:** Named university customers, a named customer quotation, a 24/7 sector SOC and public service descriptions.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Highly specific education-sector proof converges on a general sales inbox without a visible sector-specific first-call output.

## Competitor patterns
1. **Mitigo:** uses sector-specific risk content, though its own handoff is also general ([source](https://mitigogroup.com/facing-down-cyber-risk-senior-chambers-leaders-must-step-up-to-counter-a-growing-threat/)).
2. **Atlas Cloud:** carries sector specialisation into a named Cyber Security MOT ([source](https://atlascloud.co.uk/rec/)).
3. **Waterstons:** exposes named specialists beside cyber service pages ([source](https://www.waterstons.com/services/cyber/security-operations-centre-soc)).

## One hypothesis
A named Education SOC Readiness Review that retains sector/source will earn more qualified conversations than the general sales handoff.

## One proposed experiment
On education SOC collateral, test a sector-specific review CTA promising coverage map, monitoring gaps and next-step scope. Track accepted education-sector conversations.
