# Growth Leak Teardown — Cyber Tec Security

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK and Jersey certification body serving SMEs directly and through MSP partners across Cyber Essentials, Cyber Assurance and complementary security services.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit, with a more transactional entry offer than most accounts in the experiment.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Visible Solo, Guided, Cyber Essentials Plus and Assured Pass packages with starting prices, plus vulnerability assessments, incident response, penetration testing and partner packages.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** Package “Get started” actions coexist with “Request a quote” and “Speak to an Expert.”

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Package/service pages, knowledge base, blog, downloadable guides, partner programme, customer feedback, quote/contact forms, email and phone.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Certification education or package comparison → get started / quote / expert route → purchase or consultative scoping.
- **Unknown:** Which route different readiness states choose, abandonment by package and qualified cross-sell revenue.

## Proof

- **Observed:** IASME certification-body status, visible prices and inclusions, named founder/CEO Mike Ralston, extensive customer feedback and assessor-led process detail.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Clear packages reduce uncertainty, but multiple next-step types may not consistently distinguish buyers ready to purchase from those who still need help choosing.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Fortitude Cyber:** publishes starter, retainer and project scopes while keeping consultation available ([source](https://fortitudecyber.co.uk/cyber-grc/)).
2. **Quiss:** uses an assessment to recommend the right service tier ([source](https://www.quiss.co.uk/cyber-security/)).
3. **BCN:** offers one no-obligation assessment before wider managed services ([source](https://bcn.co.uk/cyber-security/cyber-security-assessment/)).

## One hypothesis

Explicitly separating “I know my package—start now” from “Help me choose—15-minute guidance” will improve both paid starts and qualified expert conversations.

## One proposed experiment

On one package-comparison surface, present only those two readiness-based actions while leaving packages, pricing and checkout unchanged. Compare package starts, expert-call quality and combined qualified conversion with the current CTA mix.
