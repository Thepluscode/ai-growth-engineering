# Growth Leak Teardown — System Force IT

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** Privately owned Gloucester MSP serving UK organisations with 10–250 employees.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** A free 60-minute audit covering cyber, M365, backups, resilience, network and support economics, followed by a written report within five working days.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Book my free IT audit.”

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Detailed audit page, service pages, resource library, local/sector positioning, phone, sales email and form.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** IT problem or resource content → broad free audit → scheduled engineer call → written report.
- **Unknown:** Trigger mix, completion, accepted opportunity and conversion by audit concern.

## Proof
- **Observed:** 20 years, ISO 27001, Microsoft Solutions Partner, 200+ leaders served and explicit delivery/eligibility details.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The offer is unusually concrete but covers several buyer jobs, potentially obscuring which trigger—security, cost, resilience or provider dissatisfaction—drives conversion.

## Competitor patterns
1. **M247:** narrows its free entry offer to M365 security ([source](https://info.m247.com/m365-security-assessment)).
2. **totality services:** uses one broad audit across services but varies surrounding page context ([source](https://www.totalityservices.co.uk/services/)).
3. **Atlas Cloud:** offers a specific board-risk MOT alongside deeper services ([source](https://atlascloud.co.uk/it-services/cyber-security-consultancy/mot/)).

## One hypothesis
Trigger-specific audit framing with unchanged delivery will improve message-match and reveal the highest-value acquisition segment.

## One proposed experiment
Create security-led and support-cost-led variants that feed the same audit, retain the trigger in CRM and compare completion, accepted opportunities and projected value.
