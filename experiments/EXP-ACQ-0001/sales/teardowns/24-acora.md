# Growth Leak Teardown — Acora

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK-headquartered technology-services group serving mid-market and enterprise organisations across managed IT, cyber security, cloud, data/AI and applications.
- **Inferred:** Fit at the upper end of this experiment's size range.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Secure 365 is a short, one-time M365 security and compliance assessment; the broader Cyber Incident Baseline assesses the wider estate from an attack-informed perspective.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Talk to a Microsoft expert” / “Get in touch” on Secure 365, plus “Baseline Your Estate” for the broader offer.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Full-stack service navigation, individual assessment pages, insights, events, news, case studies, partner proof, email, phone and contact form.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** M365 or cyber content → Secure 365 or Cyber Incident Baseline → common expert/contact route → assessment and remediation discussion.
- **Unknown:** How buyers choose between the two assessments, whether selection is retained in the contact handoff, and conversion/revenue by entry offer.

## Proof

- **Observed:** 350+ IT operations staff, 120+ cyber practitioners, 150+ cyber incidents handled in five years, all six Microsoft solution designations, detailed assessment steps and named CEO David Rabson.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Two credible adjacent assessment offers may force buyers to self-diagnose whether their problem is M365-specific or estate-wide before the sales conversation.

## Competitor patterns

1. **BCN:** explicitly tells MDR buyers to start with one free assessment that determines the right solution ([source](https://bcn.co.uk/cyber-security/managed-detection-response/)).
2. **Quiss:** uses a readiness assessment to recommend a service tier commercially and operationally ([source](https://www.quiss.co.uk/cyber-security/)).
3. **NormCyber:** leads with one quantified resilience score across its service model ([source](https://www.normcyber.com/how-it-works/cyber-resilience-score/)).

## One hypothesis

A two-choice “Microsoft 365 only” versus “whole environment” selector before the existing contact route will improve correct assessment routing and sales acceptance.

## One proposed experiment

Add the two-choice selector to Secure 365 and Cyber Incident Baseline CTAs, pass the answer into the unchanged form and compare correct first-call routing and qualified-opportunity rate with the current handoff.
