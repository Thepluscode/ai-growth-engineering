# Growth Leak Teardown — Zenzero

Research date: 2026-08-19

Replacement note: QuoStar was disqualified after its domain began redirecting to Zenzero. Zenzero is evaluated as the current live account; no QuoStar diagnosis or send is retained.

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK-headquartered managed-service provider spanning IT, cyber security, data and AI for mid-market and regulated organisations.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Broad managed and professional services plus cyber risk, MDR, SOC, penetration testing, compliance and incident response through Zensec.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** Cyber pages use “Get in touch,” “Schedule Consultation” and “Book a meeting.”

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Outcome-led navigation, service/use-case pages, case studies, news, events, contact email, telephone and meeting form.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Cyber outcome or service content → one of several generic consultation/contact labels → shared meeting/contact route.
- **Unknown:** Whether selected outcome/service is retained, source attribution, qualification, opportunity value and close rate.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Microsoft designations, B Corp, awards, quantified cases, NCSC incident-response positioning, CEO Michael Bateman and a published contact email.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The outcome-led navigation is specific, but that context may be lost when many cyber intents converge on generic consultation labels.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Chorus:** lets visitors distinguish MDR and MXDR before asking for a SOC proposal ([source](https://www.chorus.co.uk/services/cyber-security/managed-cyber-security-services/)).
2. **Intergence:** names the first-step deliverable as a custom infrastructure and breach report ([source](https://www.intergence.com/event/cybersecurity-assessment-and-infrastructure-audit)).
3. **Littlefish:** uses a named Crown Jewels analysis to frame a specific business-risk conversation ([source](https://www.littlefish.co.uk/cyber-services/)).

## One hypothesis

Retaining the chosen cyber outcome—certification, risk exposure, threat response or incident recovery—through the meeting route will produce more correctly routed qualified conversations than generic consultation CTAs.

## One proposed experiment

On the cyber overview page, test outcome-specific CTA labels that populate one hidden intent field in the existing meeting form. Compare correct routing and qualified pipeline value with the current generic labels.
