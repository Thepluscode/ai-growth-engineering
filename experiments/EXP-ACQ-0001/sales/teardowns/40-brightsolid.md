# Growth Leak Teardown — Brightsolid

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** Scotland-based managed cloud and cyber-resilience provider with an in-house SOC.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** Managed Vulnerability Scanning identifies assets and weaknesses, adds business context, prioritises remediation and reports through a secure portal.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Let's talk! Get in touch.”

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Detailed service pages, downloadable service collateral, news, partner/accreditation proof, phone, email and contact form.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** Vulnerability or cyber-resilience content → general talk/contact route → scoped conversation.
- **Unknown:** Whether asset scope, scan cadence and urgency are retained, and the service-page-to-opportunity rate.

## Proof
- **Observed:** In-house SOC, Tenable and Microsoft service detail, named reporting method, accreditations and public contact channels.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** A highly specific scanning proposition ends in a general conversation CTA, potentially dropping the scope and urgency that made the visitor high intent.

## Competitor patterns
1. **M247:** uses a free M365 assessment with score, benchmark and recommended measures ([source](https://info.m247.com/m365-security-assessment)).
2. **Central Technology:** defines a review process and findings workshop ([source](https://www.ct.co.uk/services/cyber-security-review/)).
3. **Atlas Cloud:** makes the entry assessment's output and price visible ([source](https://atlascloud.co.uk/it-services/cyber-security-consultancy/)).

## One hypothesis
An asset-scope-first CTA naming the initial vulnerability baseline will improve qualified scanning conversations.

## One proposed experiment
Test “Scope your vulnerability baseline” with asset type, approximate count and desired cadence passed into the same contact workflow. Compare qualified conversations and time-to-scope.
