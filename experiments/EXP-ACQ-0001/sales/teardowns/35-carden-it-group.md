# Growth Leak Teardown — Carden IT Group

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** UK enterprise-technology supplier and MSP with managed IT, network and security capabilities.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** A Network Assessment documenting devices, connections and vulnerabilities and feeding recommendations into wider IT strategy.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Book Your Network Assessment.”

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Service pages, assessment content, offices, company contact form, email and phone.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** IT/network content → Network Assessment → general contact route → discovery.
- **Unknown:** Whether security intent is retained, the exact deliverable, price and assessment-to-managed-service conversion.

## Proof
- **Observed:** Multiple UK offices, an established managed-services proposition and public company contact channels.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** “Network Assessment” may sound operational to buyers arriving with an explicit cyber-risk trigger, hiding the vulnerability outcome already included in delivery.

## Competitor patterns
1. **Central Technology:** leads with “Cyber Security Review” and names residual-risk outputs ([source](https://www.ct.co.uk/services/cyber-security-review/)).
2. **Atlas Cloud:** distinguishes baseline MOT, internal assessment and full reconnaissance ([source](https://atlascloud.co.uk/it-services/cyber-security-consultancy/)).
3. **AJT:** calls out a cyber posture snapshot and ranked risks ([source](https://ajt.support/our-services/free-cyber-security-health-check/)).

## One hypothesis
Security-specific framing of the existing Network Assessment will increase qualified starts from cyber pages without changing delivery.

## One proposed experiment
On cyber-relevant pages, test “Book a Network Security Assessment — map devices, connections and vulnerabilities” against the current label. Compare qualified assessment requests.
