# Growth Leak Teardown — Slink

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK technology partner for growing and regulated businesses, combining managed operations, automation/build work and specialist technical capacity through a platform-led model.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit, although the current proposition is broader than a conventional MSP.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** “Manage, Build and Scale” services, a Slink Platform for control and visibility, and a review of the buyer’s technology operation.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Talk to Slink”; a secondary “Review your technology operation” route appears near the close.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Homepage, platform and service pages, six operational stories, customer stories, pricing, insights, contact form and meeting booking.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Platform/service proof → broad conversation CTA or technology-operation review → contact/meeting route.
- **Unknown:** Which of the three service intents is captured, form completion, qualification, opportunity type and value.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Named customer testimonials, quantified customer outcomes, visible workflow examples and international operating structure.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The new three-part operating model is differentiated, but the broad “Talk to Slink” route may not preserve whether the buyer needs Manage, Build or Scale.

## Competitor patterns

1. **cyberISMS:** organises a broad portfolio around distinct IT, cyber, compliance and continuity needs ([source](https://www.cyberisms.uk/)).
2. **Fortitude Cyber:** uses explicit packages to help buyers identify the right starting scope ([source](https://fortitudecyber.co.uk/cyber-grc/)).
3. **Chorus:** separates managed-security service levels before a proposal request ([source](https://www.chorus.co.uk/services/cyber-security/managed-cyber-security-services/)).

## One hypothesis

Passing a Manage, Build or Scale choice into the existing conversation route will improve correct routing and sales acceptance without adding form friction.

## One proposed experiment

Test three one-click intent options immediately before the current contact/meeting route. Keep all downstream handling unchanged and compare correctly routed qualified conversations with the broad CTA.
