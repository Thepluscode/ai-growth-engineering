# Growth Leak Teardown — Netitude

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK MSP serving growing businesses with managed IT and cybersecurity. Founder/Managing Director Adam Harling and Client Strategy Director Michael Hamer are named on the site.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** “Growth Through Technology” positioning; Evolution managed IT tiers; managed cyber; Net9 proprietary audit; a separate general IT audit and security audit.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** Homepage “Book a free no-obligation consultation”; managed-security page “Book an IT Audit”; Net9 page “Speak to an Expert”; audit pages use “Book a call,” “Book now,” and “Enquire about an IT Audit.”

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Homepage; solution and service pages; proprietary Net9 page; multiple audit pages; sector/location pages; case studies; guides; free sessions/events; contact routes.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Multiple surfaces → one of several audit/consultation labels → enquiry or call route.
- **Unknown:** Whether these labels feed one qualification process, their relative conversion, and downstream opportunity quality.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Named customer case studies; ISO 27001/9001, Cyber Essentials/Plus, Cyber Advisor and NCSC marks; operational statistics; Net9 deliverables include evidence, compliance scores, executive summary, and strategy.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The first-step offer is strong, but its naming is fragmented across “Net9 audit,” “IT audit,” “IT security audit,” “free consultation,” and “speak to an expert.” Buyers may not understand whether these are distinct products or the same route.

## Competitor patterns

1. **The HBP Group:** uses one named 360° Assessment and one executive-level promise ([source](https://thehbpgroup.co.uk/)).
2. **Secure Chain:** consistently repeats “posture assessment” and one short enquiry form ([source](https://securechaingroup.com/)).
3. **Morcan:** names multiple assessments but explicitly states what each covers, making the difference visible ([source](https://www.morcan.co.uk/)).

## One hypothesis

Unifying the first-step language around “Net9 IT & Cyber Audit” will improve qualified booking rate by making the route and deliverable easier to understand.

## One proposed experiment

On a bounded set of managed-IT and cyber pages, replace the mixed CTA labels with “Book a Net9 IT & Cyber Audit,” followed by one sentence naming the score, report, and roadmap. Keep the underlying flow unchanged. Compare qualified booking rate with existing pages and segment results by page intent.
