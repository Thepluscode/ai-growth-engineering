# Growth Leak Teardown — Quiss

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** Privately owned UK MSP serving mid-market legal and professional-services firms with around 100 staff and nearly 35 years in operation.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Three managed cyber tiers—Shield, Armour and Fortress—supported by a Cyber Readiness Assessment that identifies gaps and recommends the appropriate commercial and operational tier.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Talk to us about which tier is right for you, or ask about our free initial assessment,” followed by the common get-in-touch form.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Cyber, managed IT and sector pages, legal-technology content, case studies, partner/accreditation proof, email, phone and embedded forms.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Service or sector page → Cyber Readiness Assessment / free initial assessment → common form → tier recommendation.
- **Unknown:** Whether assessment naming is standard across routes, whether originating sector is retained and revenue by tier/entry surface.

## Proof

- **Observed:** ISO 27001 across operations, named leadership, nearly 35 years, legal-sector specialisation, documented cases and clearly defined tier logic.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** “Cyber Readiness Assessment,” “free initial assessment” and sector-specific health-check language may divide measurement for what appears to be the same commercial first step.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Acora:** gives its bounded assessment one product name and enumerates each delivery stage ([source](https://acora.com/our-services/professional-services/secure-365-assessment-service/)).
2. **BCN:** repeats one free Cyber Security Assessment as the start of the MDR path ([source](https://bcn.co.uk/cyber-security/managed-detection-response/)).
3. **Fifosys:** names the first deliverable as an audit report with prioritised actions ([source](https://www.fifosys.com/auditing)).

## One hypothesis

Using one Cyber Readiness Assessment name while retaining sector context in the form will improve attribution and correctly scoped tier conversations.

## One proposed experiment

Canonicalise the assessment CTA on selected cyber and sector pages and pass one hidden sector field into the existing form. Compare completion, correct first-call routing and qualified tier opportunities with current variants.
