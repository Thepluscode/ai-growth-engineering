# Growth Leak Teardown — Central Technology

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** UK managed IT, cloud, communications and cyber provider with a dedicated Cyber Security Review.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** A six-stage review covering infrastructure, M365, policies, human knowledge and vulnerability testing, ending in a residual-risk report and findings workshop.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Apply now” / “Apply for your cyber security review.”

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Cyber and sector pages, educational articles, customer stories, certifications, email, phone and enquiry forms.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** Security or compliance content → review detail → application form → review process.
- **Unknown:** Price, qualification criteria, application completion, sales acceptance and review-to-project conversion.

## Proof
- **Observed:** Named customer testimony, a documented process, public accreditations and a published sales route.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** “Apply” may signal a selective or high-commitment process even though the page describes a collaborative review, potentially suppressing qualified starts.

## Competitor patterns
1. **Xperience:** frames its first step as a free under-10-minute assessment with a tailored report ([source](https://www.xperience-group.com/solutions/cyber-security/cyber-security-overview/)).
2. **CyberLab:** states time, score and expert-review outputs before the form ([source](https://cyberlab.co.uk/services/posture-assessment/)).
3. **Probrand:** lets buyers choose guided or self-serve completion ([source](https://marketplace.probrand.co.uk/campaigns/free-cyber-security-risk-assessment)).

## One hypothesis
Replacing “Apply now” with a lower-commitment, output-led CTA will increase qualified review requests without reducing fit.

## One proposed experiment
On the review page only, test “Book your Cyber Security Review — get a residual-risk report and workshop” against “Apply now.” Measure completed requests and sales-accepted reviews.
