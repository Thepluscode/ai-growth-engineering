# Growth Leak Teardown — Littlefish

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK managed IT and cyber provider serving mid-market, enterprise and public-sector organisations through managed and co-managed services.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit at the upper end of the current wedge.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Managed cyber services from UK-based security operations, security assessments and consultancy, with content including a Crown Jewels analysis.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Talk to us about managed cyber security” through a service enquiry route.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Cyber-service pages, assessment content, thought leadership, downloadable material, customer stories, frameworks, accreditations and sales telephone.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Cyber proof/content → generic managed-cyber conversation or sales enquiry. The bounded Crown Jewels analysis is visible in content rather than the main first-step CTA.
- **Unknown:** Content-to-enquiry attribution, assessment uptake, qualification, opportunity value and sales cycle.

## Proof

- **Observed:** CREST and ISO evidence, UK operations, stated incident volumes, NPS 81, CSAT 95, named customers and Cyber Services Director Sean Tickle.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** A named, bounded analysis may be easier for risk-aware buyers to act on than the generic managed-cyber conversation, but it is not currently the dominant service-page route.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Intergence:** packages consultation and technical review into a custom report ([source](https://www.intergence.com/event/cybersecurity-assessment-and-infrastructure-audit)).
2. **Technical Drive:** uses an immediate score before an expert audit ([source](https://technicaldrive.co.uk/blog/cyber-security-confidence-calculator/)).
3. **Chorus:** pairs clear MDR/MXDR explanation with a managed-SOC proposal CTA ([source](https://www.chorus.co.uk/services/cyber-security/managed-cyber-security-services/)).

## One hypothesis

Surfacing the Crown Jewels analysis as a bounded first step will produce more qualified resilience conversations than the generic managed-cyber CTA for assessment-oriented visitors.

## One proposed experiment

On one assessment-relevant cyber page, test “Map your crown jewels” against the current conversation CTA. Compare qualified assessment requests and resulting opportunity value, not raw leads.
