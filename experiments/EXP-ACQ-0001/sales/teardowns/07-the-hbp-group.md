# Growth Leak Teardown — The HBP Group

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK provider of managed IT, cybersecurity, and ERP to growth-focused SMEs with 30–500 employees. The leadership page names CEO Joanne Partridge, CCO George Smith, and CMO Phil Denham.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit; Phil Denham is the most relevant named recipient for acquisition-path feedback.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** One accountable partner across managed IT, cyber, Microsoft workplace, and ERP/accounting systems. The site describes a “360° Assessment” and Stabilise–Adopt–Enable process.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** The homepage foregrounds service-category links and general sales/enquiry contact details. The 360° Assessment is described in supporting homepage content rather than consistently presented as the primary entry CTA.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Homepage; managed-IT, cyber, ERP, and product pages; case studies/results; webinars; blog; multi-office local presence; phone/email enquiries.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Broad IT or ERP route → service exploration → general enquiry/contact. The 360° Assessment is visible as a differentiator, but the public path to initiate that specific assessment is not consistently explicit.
- **Unknown:** Surface-level conversion, cross-category confusion, qualification, pipeline, CAC, and close rate.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Named case studies and executives; 30+ years’ experience; 97% first-time-fix claim; customer outcomes tied to time saved, scale, and IT/cyber transformation; named certifications and awards.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The distinctive assessment-led first step may be buried beneath a broad combined IT-and-ERP catalogue, causing some managed-IT buyers to enter a generic enquiry path or leave without seeing the bounded diagnostic offer.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Netitude:** gives its proprietary audit a dedicated landing page, named scoring model, deliverables, and first-step status ([source](https://www.netitude.co.uk/net9)).
2. **Transputec:** separates six service pillars and uses a service-specific strategic-consultation CTA ([source](https://www.transputec.com/managed-it-services/)).
3. **Secure Chain:** pairs one assessment CTA with a short, trigger-focused form ([source](https://securechaingroup.com/)).

## One hypothesis

Making the 360° Assessment the explicit managed-IT entry offer will increase qualified IT conversations without interfering with ERP demand.

## One proposed experiment

On managed-IT pages only, test “Book your 360° IT Assessment” with a one-sentence deliverable (risks, bottlenecks, executive roadmap) against general enquiry. Route ERP pages separately. Measure sales-accepted managed-IT conversations per visitor.
