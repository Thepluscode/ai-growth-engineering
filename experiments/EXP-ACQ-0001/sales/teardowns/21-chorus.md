# Growth Leak Teardown — Chorus

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK Microsoft-focused MSP/MSSP serving SMB and mid-market organisations through managed IT, managed cyber and Microsoft consulting.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** 24/7 managed cyber security and SOC services, with separate explanations of MDR and MXDR.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Enquire now” appears early; “Request a Managed SOC Proposal” appears later.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Service pages, Microsoft/security content, resources, accreditations, customer proof, general email, enquiry form and telephone.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** MDR/MXDR and proof content → generic enquiry or managed-SOC proposal → one form asking for a free-text message.
- **Unknown:** Selected service level retained in the form, correct routing, qualification, opportunity value and close rate.

## Proof

- **Observed:** MISA/Microsoft, ISO and Cyber Essentials Plus evidence; named customers; public MTTA, MTTC and automation metrics; named CEO Nicola Saner.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Buyers are taught the difference between MDR and MXDR, but the enquiry route does not visibly preserve that choice, potentially adding avoidable qualification work.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Fortitude Cyber:** turns service maturity into explicit starter, retainer and project tiers ([source](https://fortitudecyber.co.uk/cyber-grc/)).
2. **Intergence:** defines the first-step deliverable and report before the form ([source](https://www.intergence.com/event/cybersecurity-assessment-and-infrastructure-audit)).
3. **Technical Drive:** sequences a lightweight diagnostic before the expert conversation ([source](https://technicaldrive.co.uk/blog/cyber-security-confidence-calculator/)).

## One hypothesis

A one-click MDR, MXDR or “help me scope it” choice before the existing form will improve correct routing and qualified proposal requests without reducing completion.

## One proposed experiment

Add the three-choice scope step to the managed-cyber page and pass it into the unchanged form. Compare completed qualified proposals and correct routing with the current free-text route.
