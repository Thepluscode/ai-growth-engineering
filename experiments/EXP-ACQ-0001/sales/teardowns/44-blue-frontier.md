# Growth Leak Teardown — Blue Frontier

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** UK digital-growth partner with in-house IT, cyber, development, marketing, research and creative services.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit for the provider wedge and especially relevant to the wider Digital Marketing Project.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** Managed vulnerability assessment, patching and security-compliance support delivered inside a broad IT and cyber practice.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Discuss a project.”

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** IT/cyber service pages, digital-marketing pages, work, whitepapers, multi-office presence, phone and project form.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** Discipline-specific content → portfolio-wide “Discuss a project” route → discovery.
- **Unknown:** Whether selected discipline and service persist into routing, qualification and attribution.

## Proof
- **Observed:** Operating since 2005, named work and testimony, multi-discipline in-house teams, ISO evidence and public procurement credentials.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** A common project CTA across a very broad portfolio may discard the precise cyber intent created by the vulnerability-control page.

## Competitor patterns
1. **Brightsolid:** uses a service-specific vulnerability-scanning proposition with explicit reporting detail ([source](https://brightsolid.com/services/managed-vulnerability-scanning/)).
2. **M247:** offers an assessment-specific first step and score ([source](https://info.m247.com/m365-security-assessment)).
3. **Central Technology:** names the review output and workshop before contact ([source](https://www.ct.co.uk/services/cyber-security-review/)).

## One hypothesis
Carrying the selected cyber service into the project enquiry will improve correct routing and accepted cyber opportunities.

## One proposed experiment
On IT/cyber pages, test “Discuss your vulnerability baseline” with service/source preselected against “Discuss a project.” Compare routing accuracy and accepted opportunities.
