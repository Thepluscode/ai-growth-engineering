# Growth Leak Teardown — Air IT

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK SME-focused managed technology group with a dedicated Air Sec division and eight UK offices.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit, although larger than the earliest founder-led accounts.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** End-to-end cyber security spanning managed SIEM/SOC, MDR, network security, vulnerability management, Cyber Essentials, penetration testing, awareness, email and identity protection.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Get in touch” is repeated across the cyber page; the footer reframes it as a free consultation.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Cyber and service pages, sector pages, resource content, case studies, accreditations, quote page, enquiry email, telephone and contact form.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Specific cyber service or educational content → “Get in touch” → general contact route.
- **Unknown:** Whether the selected service is retained, how enquiries are routed, qualification rate and revenue by originating service.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Dedicated certified Air Sec team, eight UK offices, ISO 27001 and Cyber Essentials Plus positioning, named technology partners and public customer evidence.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** A buyer can express intent across many distinct services, but the visible handoff does not show that intent being retained, potentially adding manual qualification and obscuring service-level conversion.

## Competitor patterns

1. **BCN:** routes MDR visitors explicitly into one free Cyber Security Assessment ([source](https://bcn.co.uk/cyber-security/managed-detection-response/)).
2. **ramsac:** names a Cyber Resilience Assessment and explains the resulting benchmark/certification ([source](https://www.ramsac.com/managed-cybersecurity-services/)).
3. **Quiss:** uses one assessment to recommend the commercially appropriate security tier ([source](https://www.quiss.co.uk/cyber-security/)).

## One hypothesis

Passing the selected cyber service into the existing contact route will increase correctly routed qualified conversations without increasing form friction.

## One proposed experiment

Add one hidden source field populated from the originating cyber service page and display that selection in the form confirmation. Compare correct first-time routing and qualified-conversation rate with the current general handoff.
