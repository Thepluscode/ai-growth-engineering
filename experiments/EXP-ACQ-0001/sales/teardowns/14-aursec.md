# Growth Leak Teardown — Aursec

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK SMB cybersecurity consultancy covering Cyber Essentials/Plus, managed security, penetration testing, data protection and training.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Certification and security services supported by a four-step scoping, gap-assessment, remediation and certification process.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** Repeated free 30-minute scoping call.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Homepage, service pages, tools and guides, a short security health-check route, testimonials and contact routes.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Service content → scoping call; a lower-friction health-check tool exists as a separate acquisition surface.
- **Unknown:** Health-check prominence, completion, handoff to a call, qualification and revenue conversion.

## Proof

- **Observed:** NCSC-aligned positioning, certified-assessor claims, a named client testimonial and a defined delivery process.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Visitors who are not ready for a 30-minute call may not encounter the lower-friction diagnostic at the moment of highest service-page intent.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Technical Drive:** puts a two-minute personalised score before the audit conversation ([source](https://technicaldrive.co.uk/blog/cyber-security-confidence-calculator/)).
2. **Intergence:** defines the assessment output as a custom infrastructure and breach report ([source](https://www.intergence.com/event/cybersecurity-assessment-and-infrastructure-audit)).
3. **Fortitude Cyber:** identifies a low-cost starting package and its exact deliverables ([source](https://fortitudecyber.co.uk/cyber-grc/)).

## One hypothesis

Offering the security health check beside the scoping call on one high-intent service page will create more qualified conversations from lower-readiness visitors without reducing direct bookings.

## One proposed experiment

On the Cyber Essentials page only, test a secondary “Check readiness first” CTA to the existing tool. Track tool starts, completions and qualified calls while retaining the current scoping-call control.
