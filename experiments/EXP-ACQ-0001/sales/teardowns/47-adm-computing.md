# Growth Leak Teardown — ADM Computing

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** Family-owned Kent MSP providing managed IT, cybersecurity, Microsoft and infrastructure services.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** A free cyber-security audit for organisations assessing their current security posture.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Request a free cyber security audit.”

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Homepage, service pages, events, customer reviews, free audit form, sales email and phone.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** IT/cyber content → audit form asking contact, company, users and postcode → advisor response.
- **Unknown:** The audit's exact output, completion rate, sales acceptance and audit-to-opportunity conversion.

## Proof
- **Observed:** 75+ staff, 10,000 supported users, 450 technical certifications, 99.8%+ satisfaction, named reviews and long operating history.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The form asks for meaningful qualification data before the public page explains the audit deliverable, potentially weakening the value exchange.

## Competitor patterns
1. **System Force IT:** promises a written report in five working days before its form ([source](https://systemforce.co.uk/free-it-audit/)).
2. **CyberLab:** names ten domains, a score and expert review ([source](https://cyberlab.co.uk/services/posture-assessment/)).
3. **Central Technology:** shows the complete review process and report/workshop outputs ([source](https://www.ct.co.uk/services/cyber-security-review/)).

## One hypothesis
Naming the audit duration and take-away before the form will increase qualified completions without reducing lead quality.

## One proposed experiment
Test an output-led audit panel—scope, time required and take-away—above the existing form. Compare form completion and sales-accepted audit rate.
