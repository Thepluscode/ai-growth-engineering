# Growth Leak Teardown — Foresight IT Services

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** Greater Manchester MSP providing managed IT, cyber, communications and strategic services to local organisations.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** A free IT Assessment sits alongside a Cyber Risk Calculator and Cyber Essentials Readiness tool.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Book a Free IT Assessment,” with separate free-tool routes.

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Local landing pages, service pages, free tools, cases, reviews, live chat, sales email, phone and forms.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** Local/service content → free IT assessment or one of two cyber tools → sales contact.
- **Unknown:** Overlap, path attribution, result persistence and accepted-opportunity rate by route.

## Proof
- **Observed:** Current public contact channels, Microsoft/Cyber Essentials positioning, local service detail, reviews and stated support scope.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Three adjacent no-cost entry routes may fragment intent and make it difficult to identify which proposition creates qualified conversations.

## Competitor patterns
1. **Probrand:** treats guided and self-serve as modes of one canonical risk assessment ([source](https://marketplace.probrand.co.uk/campaigns/free-cyber-security-risk-assessment)).
2. **CyberLab:** labels external risk and internal posture as distinct jobs ([source](https://cyberlab.co.uk/services/consultancy/)).
3. **OryxAlign:** distinguishes quick score from expert maturity work ([source](https://www.oryxalign.com/cyber)).

## One hypothesis
A single “choose your starting point” layer with clear suitability rules will improve correct selection and attribution.

## One proposed experiment
Route all free-entry CTAs through a three-choice panel, persist the choice and compare completions plus accepted opportunities by tool.
