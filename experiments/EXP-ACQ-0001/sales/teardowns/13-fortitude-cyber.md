# Growth Leak Teardown — Fortitude Cyber

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK cyber and GRC consultancy serving SMEs that need Cyber Essentials, ISO 27001, risk management or fractional security leadership.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** A £695 starter pack, £395–£995 monthly GRC retainers, £4,995–£8,995 compliance projects and separately priced add-ons.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Request a free consultation”; the package page also says most SMBs begin with Tier 1 or a readiness review.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Homepage, service and sector pages, detailed package/pricing page, articles and published enquiry email.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Problem/service content → package guidance → free consultation or email.
- **Unknown:** Package-page traffic, tier selection, qualified-conversation rate, sales cycle and close rate.

## Proof

- **Observed:** 15+ years of stated experience, recognised-framework positioning, explicit deliverables, delivery times and prices.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The unusually clear paid starter offer may lose some of its buying momentum when the final action reverts to a generic free consultation.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Slink:** connects a named readiness review to gaps, priorities and a practical plan ([source](https://slink.co.uk/capabilities-managed-it)).
2. **Intergence:** specifies consultation followed by a custom assessment report ([source](https://www.intergence.com/event/cybersecurity-assessment-and-infrastructure-audit)).
3. **Aursec:** explains a four-step scoping, gap, remediation and certification process ([source](https://www.aursec.co.uk/)).

## One hypothesis

A direct “Start Tier 1” route that confirms fit before booking will generate more paid, qualified starts than sending all package-aware buyers to a free consultation.

## One proposed experiment

On the package page only, test “Start Tier 1” beside the existing consultation CTA. Ask company size, target standard and deadline, then route accepted fits to a short call. Compare paid starts and qualified calls per unique visitor.
