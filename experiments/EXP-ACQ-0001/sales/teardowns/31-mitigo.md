# Growth Leak Teardown — Mitigo

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK cyber-risk management provider combining independent assessment, technical controls and ongoing assurance for professional-services and other regulated organisations.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Business-focused cyber risk assessment, control programme and ongoing assurance positioned as independent from the client's IT provider.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** Sector and educational pages ask visitors to complete their details so Mitigo can discuss their needs; the contact page uses one general form.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Sector articles and guides, professional-body relationships, webinars/resources, board biographies, direct email, phone and contact forms.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Sector-specific risk content → general “speak to Mitigo” form → needs discussion.
- **Unknown:** Whether sector/source is retained, the precise first-call deliverable, qualification criteria and opportunity economics.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Named board with legal, technical and commercial expertise, public professional-sector material, partner/association positioning and a defined independent-risk rationale.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** High-specificity sector content converges on a general needs form without visibly naming the assessment output, potentially losing the context that made the visitor high intent.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Quiss:** carries legal/professional-services specialisation into a named Cyber Readiness Assessment ([source](https://www.quiss.co.uk/cyber-security/)).
2. **BCN:** defines the free assessment output before the booking form ([source](https://bcn.co.uk/cyber-security/cyber-security-assessment/)).
3. **Fifosys:** specifies a report, risk priorities and action plan as the audit output ([source](https://www.fifosys.com/auditing)).

## One hypothesis

Naming a sector-specific Cyber Risk Baseline and retaining the originating sector in the existing form will improve qualified assessment conversations without requiring a new service.

## One proposed experiment

On one professional-services content cluster, replace the generic discussion CTA with “Request your [sector] Cyber Risk Baseline,” state the three outputs and pass sector/source into the same form. Compare qualified assessment requests and correct routing.
