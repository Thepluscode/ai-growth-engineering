# Growth Leak Teardown — CyberLab

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** UK cyber specialist providing consultancy, testing, managed protection and compliance services.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** A free guided Posture Assessment across ten NCSC-aligned domains and a separate free HackRisk external-risk report.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Book Free Consultation,” “Get Free HackRisk Report” and “Speak to Cyber Expert.”

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Consultancy and solution pages, two free offers, reports, blog, case studies, email, phone and forms.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** Risk/service content → Posture Assessment, HackRisk report or expert conversation → contact capture and review.
- **Unknown:** Cross-offer attribution, overlap, buyer self-selection and each offer's accepted-opportunity rate.

## Proof
- **Observed:** Named customer evidence, NCSC-aligned methodology, CREST/CHECK positioning and quantified specialist experience.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Two strong free entry offers may appear interchangeable before buyers understand internal control maturity versus external exposure.

## Competitor patterns
1. **Atlas Cloud:** makes assessment depth a visible ladder ([source](https://atlascloud.co.uk/it-services/cyber-security-consultancy/)).
2. **Probrand:** labels guided and self-serve as completion modes for one canonical assessment ([source](https://marketplace.probrand.co.uk/campaigns/free-cyber-security-risk-assessment)).
3. **OryxAlign:** positions a quick score separately from an in-depth maturity roadmap ([source](https://www.oryxalign.com/cyber)).

## One hypothesis
Explicitly labelling HackRisk as “outside-in exposure” and Posture Assessment as “inside-out controls” will improve correct offer selection and attribution.

## One proposed experiment
Test a two-card chooser with those labels and one-sentence suitability rules. Retain selected offer in CRM and compare completion plus accepted-opportunity rate.
