# Growth Leak Teardown — Xperience

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** UK technology partner offering managed IT, cloud and specialist cyber services.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** A free assessment taking under ten minutes and returning a customised report across five cyber-resilience pillars.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Start your free Cyber Security Assessment” / “Get Started.”

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Service pages, assessment content, case studies, articles, multiple offices, phone and a segmented contact form.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** Cyber content → free assessment → customised report → expert availability or general cyber enquiry.
- **Unknown:** Whether the score and weakest pillar are retained in CRM, expert-contact rate and opportunity conversion.

## Proof
- **Observed:** Public case studies, stated 95% recommendation, over 50 years' experience and specialist cyber-team experience.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The assessment creates high-value intent data, but the public journey does not show a score-aware next step, potentially forcing the buyer to restart context in a generic enquiry.

## Competitor patterns
1. **CyberLab:** explicitly includes an expert review as step three of its assessment ([source](https://cyberlab.co.uk/services/posture-assessment/)).
2. **BCN:** names the report and follow-up discussion inside the assessment proposition ([source](https://bcn.co.uk/cyber-security/cyber-security-assessment/)).
3. **OryxAlign:** emails the result and advice, creating a defined follow-up object ([source](https://info.oryxalign.com/cyber-security-score)).

## One hypothesis
A result-aware review CTA naming the buyer's weakest pillar will earn more qualified expert conversations than a generic post-report contact route.

## One proposed experiment
For assessment completers, test “Review your [weakest pillar] result with a cyber expert” against the current next step. Measure booked reviews and sales-accepted opportunities.
