# Growth Leak Teardown — Atlas Cloud

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** Independent UK managed IT and cyber provider serving professional-services and other security-conscious organisations.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** A 12-point Cyber Security MOT with certificate, health report and expert review, plus deeper internal assessment, reconnaissance and penetration testing.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Book a Consultation,” “Schedule yours” and “Make enquiry.”

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Cyber service ladder, sector pages, proprietary MOT, research, cases, transparent metrics, email, phone and forms.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** Sector/cyber content → MOT or deeper assessment choice → enquiry/consultation → review.
- **Unknown:** Selection accuracy, progression between levels and accepted-opportunity rate by entry point.

## Proof
- **Observed:** Thousands of companies audited, named leadership and clients, ISO accreditations and published service outputs and prices.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Step 1, Step 2 and reconnaissance share similar starting language and pricing, potentially making depth selection harder than necessary.

## Competitor patterns
1. **Probrand:** separates guided and self-serve modes for one defined report ([source](https://marketplace.probrand.co.uk/campaigns/free-cyber-security-risk-assessment)).
2. **CyberLab:** gives one broad free posture assessment an explicit three-step process ([source](https://cyberlab.co.uk/services/posture-assessment/)).
3. **OryxAlign:** distinguishes a quick score from a full maturity roadmap ([source](https://www.oryxalign.com/cyber)).

## One hypothesis
Suitability rules based on desired assurance and access level will increase correct assessment selection and reduce re-scoping.

## One proposed experiment
Add a three-question chooser before the consultancy ladder and pass the recommended level into the existing enquiry form. Measure correct routing and time-to-scope.
