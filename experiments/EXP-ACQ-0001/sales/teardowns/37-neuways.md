# Growth Leak Teardown — Neuways

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** Derby-based UK provider of managed IT, Business Central and managed cyber security.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** Threatsafe combines vulnerability assessment, managed detection, awareness, network/email security and continuity support.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Become Threatsafe,” “Be Threatsafe,” “Let's Talk” and “Get In Touch.”

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Managed-cyber page, service pages, weekly threat content, client cases, email, business-development phone and form.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** Threat or service content → branded Threatsafe proposition → one general contact form.
- **Unknown:** The first-call deliverable, retained service intent, qualification and programme-to-opportunity conversion.

## Proof
- **Observed:** Public client cases, named outcomes, over 30 years' collective cyber experience and published commercial channels.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Threatsafe covers many buyer problems but the public next step does not define a bounded starting output, potentially asking prospects to scope the programme themselves.

## Competitor patterns
1. **Central Technology:** starts with a bounded review and residual-risk report ([source](https://www.ct.co.uk/services/cyber-security-review/)).
2. **CyberLab:** defines a free score and expert review before ongoing services ([source](https://cyberlab.co.uk/services/posture-assessment/)).
3. **System Force IT:** promises a written, prioritised report on a fixed timeline ([source](https://systemforce.co.uk/free-it-audit/)).

## One hypothesis
Naming a Threatsafe Baseline Review with three explicit outputs will improve qualified starts over the broad “Let's Talk” route.

## One proposed experiment
On the managed-cyber page, test a single “Request your Threatsafe Baseline” CTA with risk summary, priority controls and next-step plan. Measure accepted cyber conversations.
