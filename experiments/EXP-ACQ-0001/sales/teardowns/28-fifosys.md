# Growth Leak Teardown — Fifosys

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK MSP/MSSP serving SMEs and mid-market organisations across managed/co-managed IT, cyber security, cloud, infrastructure and compliance.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Managed services begin with an IT Audit covering performance, vulnerabilities and business alignment, followed by a consultation, onboarding and implementation.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Ready to Discover Stress-Free IT?” / “Get in touch with our team”; a dedicated auditing page also offers an online IT Audit questionnaire.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Managed-service and audit pages, sector routes, case studies, guides, video, awards/accreditations, general email, telephone and forms.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** General or sector service page → contact/audit → report with prioritised recommendations → consultation → onboarding.
- **Unknown:** How prominently the audit is seen before contact, questionnaire completion and qualified-opportunity value by route.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Explicit four-step process, detailed report output, ISO 27001 since 2013, Cyber Essentials Plus, ten MSP 501 appearances, named customers and founder/MD Mitesh Patel.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The audit is a concrete and differentiated first step, but the initial managed-services CTA describes the desired feeling rather than the deliverable, so some buyers may miss the lowest-friction route.

## Competitor patterns

1. **BCN:** leads with a free assessment and spells out the risk/recommendation output ([source](https://bcn.co.uk/cyber-security/cyber-security-assessment/)).
2. **Acora:** positions a short one-time engagement and lists five delivery steps ([source](https://acora.com/our-services/professional-services/secure-365-assessment-service/)).
3. **Quiss:** makes the assessment's commercial role explicit: recommend the right tier ([source](https://www.quiss.co.uk/cyber-security/)).

## One hypothesis

Leading with the audit report and action plan as the first CTA will create more qualified audit conversations than a generic stress-free-IT prompt.

## One proposed experiment

Replace only the hero CTA on a controlled managed-services page variant with “Start with an IT Audit—get a prioritised action plan.” Keep form and delivery unchanged; compare audit starts and qualified opportunities.
