# Growth Leak Teardown — ramsac

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK managed IT and cybersecurity provider with more than 30 years' experience and an SME/organisation-focused proposition.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Managed cyber services, secure+, Cyber Essentials, consultancy and training, anchored by a Cyber Resilience Assessment and Bronze/Silver/Gold resilience certification.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** Consultation/contact prompts and a no-obligation assessment of the current IT setup.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Managed-cyber pages, totalIT tiers, board training, events, books/speaking, blog, certifications, contact form and telephone.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Educational or service content → assessment/consultation → estate benchmark → certification or recommended managed service.
- **Unknown:** Which label initiates the most qualified demand, how tier recommendation is presented, and downstream economics.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Cyber Essentials Plus, ISO 27001/9001, named cyber specialists, founder authority, 30+ years and a defined benchmark/certification method.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Buyers encounter assessment, certification, secure+ and totalIT tier concepts before a conversation; the number of labels may obscure the single best starting action.

## Competitor patterns

1. **BCN:** positions one free Cyber Security Assessment as the explicit start for wider services ([source](https://bcn.co.uk/cyber-security/cyber-security-assessment/)).
2. **Quiss:** makes its readiness assessment the mechanism that selects the right tier ([source](https://www.quiss.co.uk/cyber-security/)).
3. **Cyber Tec:** separates self-serve, guided and Plus packages with visible starting prices ([source](https://www.cybertecsecurity.com/)).

## One hypothesis

Making the Cyber Resilience Assessment the single entry CTA, with the tier recommendation stated as its output, will increase qualified assessment requests without changing service delivery.

## One proposed experiment

Replace competing first-step labels on selected managed-cyber pages with “Start the Cyber Resilience Assessment—receive your benchmark and recommended tier.” Compare qualified assessment starts and expectation-match against current pages.
