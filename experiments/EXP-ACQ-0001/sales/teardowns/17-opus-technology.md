# Growth Leak Teardown — Opus Technology

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK managed technology provider serving large SMEs, mid-market, enterprise and public-sector organisations across IT, cyber, communications and adjacent services.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Cyber consultancy, XDR, IAM, dark-web monitoring, backup, phishing protection and managed IT within a broad technology portfolio.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Speak to a consultant.”

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Service pages, customer stories, insight content, broad solution navigation, telephone and consultation routes.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Service/proof content → consultant CTA → general consultation route.
- **Unknown:** Service intent retained in the handoff, qualified-conversation rate, opportunity mix, CAC and close rate.

## Proof

- **Observed:** 1,000+ stated customers, 97.5% CSAT, 99.2% P1 first-time fix, B Corp status, customer evidence and named leadership.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Specific cyber triggers may become less concrete when they converge into the same consultant CTA used across a wide multi-service portfolio.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Chorus:** distinguishes MDR from MXDR and offers a managed-SOC proposal ([source](https://www.chorus.co.uk/services/cyber-security/managed-cyber-security-services/)).
2. **Littlefish:** surfaces a bounded Crown Jewels analysis within its cyber content ([source](https://www.littlefish.co.uk/cyber-services/)).
3. **Intergence:** defines a report-producing infrastructure and breach assessment ([source](https://www.intergence.com/event/cybersecurity-assessment-and-infrastructure-audit)).

## One hypothesis

A cyber-specific “Security Exposure Review” with one stated output will generate more qualified cyber conversations than the broad “Speak to a consultant” CTA.

## One proposed experiment

On the cyber overview page only, test the bounded review against the existing CTA while keeping the same sales team and form. Compare qualified cyber opportunities and pipeline value per visitor.
