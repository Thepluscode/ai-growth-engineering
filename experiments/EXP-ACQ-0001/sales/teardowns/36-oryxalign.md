# Growth Leak Teardown — OryxAlign

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** London B Corp MSP serving UK SMEs and mid-market organisations across managed IT, cyber and cloud.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** A 14-question Cyber Security Score, an in-depth maturity assessment and a tailored cyber assessment/audit.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Get your cyber security score,” “Get in touch” and a 45-minute booking route.

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Score tool, cyber/service pages, sector pages, customer stories, guides, email, phone, form and calendar.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** Content/service interest → score, maturity assessment or tailored-audit route → email/form/calendar.
- **Unknown:** How those entry labels map to one commercial journey and whether score data reaches the booked conversation.

## Proof
- **Observed:** Named client testimony, B Corp status, public service detail and a current company summary with contact information.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Three adjacent assessment labels may make buyers choose a method before they know which depth fits their situation.

## Competitor patterns
1. **CyberLab:** distinguishes an internal posture assessment from external HackRisk monitoring ([source](https://cyberlab.co.uk/services/consultancy/)).
2. **Atlas Cloud:** presents a visible depth ladder from MOT to reconnaissance and penetration test ([source](https://atlascloud.co.uk/it-services/cyber-security-consultancy/)).
3. **Xperience:** uses one fast assessment as the repeated cyber entry point ([source](https://www.xperience-group.com/solutions/cyber-security/cyber-security-overview/)).

## One hypothesis
A simple “quick score” versus “expert maturity review” choice will improve correct routing and qualified-conversation rate.

## One proposed experiment
Unify cyber assessment CTAs behind a two-choice panel, retain the selected route in the form/calendar and compare correct routing plus accepted opportunities.
