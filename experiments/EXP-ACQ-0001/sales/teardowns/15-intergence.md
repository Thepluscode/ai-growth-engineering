# Growth Leak Teardown — Intergence

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** Cambridge-headquartered IT consultancy and MSP offering managed IT, managed security, cloud, consultancy and CISO-as-a-Service.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Managed and consulting services plus a free infrastructure and cyber-breach assessment involving consultation, dark-web/architecture review and a custom report.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Talk to Sales” on contact pages; service journeys also use “free assessment,” “free consultation,” “Get in touch” and report CTAs.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Service pages, assessment landing page, breach-awareness report, case studies, partner pages, articles, telephone, email and sales form.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Cyber/managed-service content → one of several assessment or consultation labels → sales form or contact route.
- **Unknown:** Cross-page attribution, visitor comprehension, form completion, qualification and opportunity value.

## Proof

- **Observed:** 20+ years of history, certifications, named executive team, customer evidence and measurable-outcomes positioning.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Several overlapping free-assessment, consultation and report labels may fragment measurement and make the expected first-step output unclear.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Netitude:** centres one named Net9 audit with a score, report and roadmap ([source](https://www.netitude.co.uk/net9)).
2. **Slink:** frames one review around gaps, priorities and a practical plan ([source](https://slink.co.uk/)).
3. **Technical Drive:** sequences a score first and a free audit second ([source](https://technicaldrive.co.uk/blog/cyber-security-confidence-calculator/)).

## One hypothesis

One consistent “Infrastructure & Breach Exposure Report” label and deliverable will generate more qualified assessment requests than the current mix of free-entry labels.

## One proposed experiment

Use that single label on managed-security and managed-services pages while preserving the existing form. Pass the source page into the CRM and compare qualified requests and correct routing with the current labels.
