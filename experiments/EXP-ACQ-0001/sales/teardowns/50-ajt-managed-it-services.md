# Growth Leak Teardown — AJT Managed IT Services

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** Brighton and Sussex MSP serving SMEs with managed IT, cyber and Microsoft 365.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** A free, remote cyber health check returning a plain-English posture snapshot, ranked risks and an optional findings call.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** Apply for the free health check through a form; the review call is described as optional.

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Health-check page, local service pages, extensive named cases, testimonials, email, phone and form.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** Local/cyber content → health-check form → concise report → optional review call.
- **Unknown:** Report completion, review booking, qualification and opportunity conversion.

## Proof
- **Observed:** Named client cases, 60%+ cost reduction in one engagement, operational outcomes, named leadership and published contact routes.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Treating the result-review call as an optional afterthought may miss the moment when a prospect's ranked risk is most commercially salient.

## Competitor patterns
1. **CyberLab:** makes expert review a formal third assessment step ([source](https://cyberlab.co.uk/services/posture-assessment/)).
2. **Central Technology:** includes a findings workshop in the review process ([source](https://www.ct.co.uk/services/cyber-security-review/)).
3. **Atlas Cloud:** presents results in a defined 15-minute appointment ([source](https://atlascloud.co.uk/it-services/cyber-security-consultancy/)).

## One hypothesis
A result-aware review CTA presented with the top ranked risk will increase qualified conversations without making the health check feel sales-led.

## One proposed experiment
On report delivery, test “Review your #1 risk in 15 minutes” against the passive optional-call copy. Measure booked reviews and accepted opportunities.
