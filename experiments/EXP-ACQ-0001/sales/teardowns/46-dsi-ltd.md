# Growth Leak Teardown — DSI Ltd

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** Independent UK IT solutions and managed-services provider spanning cloud, infrastructure, security and networking.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** A managed-services promotion offers one free month and a free Cyber Security Assessment when signing a qualifying contract.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Sign Up Now.”

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Promotion page, managed-service collateral, company pages, solution content, UK sales email, phone and form.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** Promotion → form → managed-services sales conversation.
- **Unknown:** Current traffic, form completion, whether submissions are still accepted and downstream opportunity economics.

## Proof
- **Observed:** Public company history, independent ownership, detailed capabilities, named markets and published sales channels.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** On 19 August 2026 the live campaign still states that the promotion ends 31 July 2026, potentially reducing confidence or creating uncertainty about the offer.

## Competitor patterns
1. **System Force IT:** uses an evergreen, no-obligation audit with fixed outputs ([source](https://systemforce.co.uk/free-it-audit/)).
2. **CyberLab:** maintains a current free assessment with an explicit process ([source](https://cyberlab.co.uk/services/posture-assessment/)).
3. **Probrand:** gives current guided and self-serve assessment routes ([source](https://marketplace.probrand.co.uk/campaigns/free-cyber-security-risk-assessment)).

## One hypothesis
Retiring or updating the expired promotion and offering an evergreen assessment will improve trusted starts from the page.

## One proposed experiment
Redirect the expired campaign to an evergreen “Managed Services Security Baseline” with current terms and the same form. Compare qualified submissions against the expired page's baseline.
