# Growth Leak Teardown — NormCyber

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK MSSP serving mid-market and enterprise organisations across MDR, continuous exposure management, incident response, GRC and data protection.
- **Inferred:** Fit at the enterprise end of the experiment.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Managed security anchored by a Cyber Resilience Score out of 100, a named Focal Analyst and the Smartbloc portal, with a CREST-accredited 24/7 UK SOC.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Get resilient,” “Book a demo,” “Book a 15-minute consultation” and service-specific meeting prompts.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Score, Focal Analyst, portal and service pages; customer success; reports; news; events; accreditations; contact form; email; phone and live chat.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Differentiated concept or service page → demo/consultation/contact → managed-security discussion.
- **Unknown:** Whether the originating concept is carried into the conversation, funnel economics by concept and sales acceptance.

## Proof

- **Observed:** NCSC CAF-based score, CREST-accredited SOC, named Focal Analyst process, detailed customer outcomes, awards, named CEO Wayne Churchill and public performance claims tied to cases.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The proposition is strongly differentiated, but several CTA labels may hide which concept—score, analyst, portal or SOC—created the buyer's intent and therefore weaken routing or attribution.

## Competitor patterns

1. **BCN:** sends MDR interest into one explicitly named assessment route ([source](https://bcn.co.uk/cyber-security/managed-detection-response/)).
2. **ramsac:** connects assessment, benchmark and certification in one named journey ([source](https://www.ramsac.com/managed-cybersecurity-services/)).
3. **Quiss:** uses the assessment output to select a service tier ([source](https://www.quiss.co.uk/cyber-security/)).

## One hypothesis

Making “Baseline your Cyber Resilience Score” the consistent first action while preserving the originating service will improve correctly scoped qualified meetings and concept-level attribution.

## One proposed experiment

Use that single CTA on selected score, Focal Analyst and SOC pages and pass page intent into the existing booking route. Compare correct first-call routing and qualified meetings against current CTA variants.
