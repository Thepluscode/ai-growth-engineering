# Growth Leak Teardown — BCN Group

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK Microsoft-focused technology partner serving medium-sized businesses across managed IT, cloud, business applications and cyber security.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** A free Cyber Security Assessment that reviews current posture, highlights risks and provides recommendations, followed by implementation or ongoing cyber services where required.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Book now” / “Book Your Free Cyber Security Assessment”; another live page calls the same route a “Free Secure Score Assessment.”

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Cyber and MDR pages, sector pages, Microsoft content, resources, assessment landing pages, leadership content, email, phone and forms.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Service or sector page → free Cyber Security Assessment / Secure Score Assessment → booking form → recommendations → optional implementation or managed service.
- **Unknown:** Whether both assessment names represent an identical scope, whether their attribution is unified, and downstream opportunity value.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Microsoft Solutions Partner with all six designations, named CEO Rob Davies, defined assessment outputs and an end-to-end route from assessment through managed protection.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** Two names for what appears to be one free entry offer may fragment attribution or set different buyer expectations even though the underlying funnel is otherwise strong.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Acora:** gives its bounded M365 assessment one stable product name—Secure 365—and lists five delivery steps ([source](https://acora.com/our-services/professional-services/secure-365-assessment-service/)).
2. **ramsac:** connects a named assessment to a visible certification outcome ([source](https://www.ramsac.com/managed-cybersecurity-services/)).
3. **Fifosys:** defines Audit, Consultation and Onboarding as explicit successive steps ([source](https://www.fifosys.com/managed-services)).

## One hypothesis

Using one assessment name and one shared attribution definition across service and sector pages will improve assessment-to-qualified-opportunity measurement without reducing bookings.

## One proposed experiment

Canonicalise the CTA and confirmation copy to “Free BCN Cyber Security Assessment” on half of eligible pages while keeping scope, form and traffic unchanged. Compare form completion, expectation-match at first call and qualified-opportunity rate.
