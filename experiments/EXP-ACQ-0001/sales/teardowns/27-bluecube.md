# Growth Leak Teardown — Bluecube

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** UK managed IT and communications provider offering cyber security, connectivity, mobile and unified communications from London and the Midlands.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** A free IT & Communications audit assessing infrastructure, licensing, security, support and communications, with a report covering improvements and cost savings.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** “Book a call” / “Get in touch”; the audit page embeds a multi-field form with CAPTCHA.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** IT, cyber and communications pages, free-audit form, reviews, cases, partner proof, articles, contact form, email, phone, live chat and WhatsApp.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Broad service or proof page → free IT & Communications audit / contact → CAPTCHA form or conversation → report and recommendation.
- **Unknown:** Whether a cyber-origin visitor receives a security-specific scope, form abandonment and qualified opportunities by intent.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Named Managing Director and Sales Director, public customer cases/reviews, stated 9.7 CSAT and two UK offices.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The broad audit is commercially useful, but a cyber-intent visitor may not immediately recognise that security is a specific output, potentially lowering relevance before the form.

## Competitor patterns

1. **BCN:** offers a cyber-only assessment with security posture, gaps and recommendations defined ([source](https://bcn.co.uk/cyber-security/cyber-security-assessment/)).
2. **Acora:** scopes its assessment specifically to Microsoft 365 security and compliance ([source](https://acora.com/our-services/professional-services/secure-365-assessment-service/)).
3. **Fifosys:** states the audit report structure and prioritisation logic before contact ([source](https://www.fifosys.com/auditing)).

## One hypothesis

A cyber-specific presentation of the existing audit on cyber pages will improve qualified security conversations without creating a new delivery product.

## One proposed experiment

On selected cyber pages, relabel the same form “Free Cyber & IT Risk Audit” and show its security outputs while keeping backend process and fields unchanged. Compare completed forms and qualified cyber opportunities with the broad audit framing.
