# Growth Leak Teardown — TWC IT Solutions

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit

- **Observed:** London-headquartered MSP offering IT support and cybersecurity to scaling SMEs; the site reports 300+ clients and substantial UK/South East coverage.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit. CEO Paolo Sartori is a named commercial decision-maker and is described as hands-on with clients.

## Current offer

- **Observed:** Cybersecurity, managed IT support, disaster recovery, connectivity, contact centre, unified communications, and systems integration. Cybersecurity includes endpoint security, SOC/DDoS, managed firewall, audit/consultancy, MDM, and GDPR.

## Primary CTA

- **Observed:** Homepage “Contact us”; cybersecurity page directs visitors to a quick-contact form, phone, or social channel.

## Acquisition surfaces

- **Observed:** Homepage; solution-category pages; cybersecurity page; case-study PDFs; awards/distinctions; contact form; phone and sales email.

## Observable funnel

- **Observed:** Homepage or service page → contact page → long form containing multiple product families and many selectable services → send.
- **Unknown:** Abandonment, enquiry quality, response time, opportunity conversion, CAC, and close rate.

## Proof

- **Observed:** Site reports 300+ clients, 400 cities, 24 countries, NPS 90, a 10-minute SLA, 110+ distinctions, and a seamless-transition warranty.
- **Observed:** The Mallowstreet case study describes a security audit followed by phased remediation.

## Likely commercial leak

- **Inference, not diagnosis:** A high-intent cybersecurity visitor may face excess choice and form friction because the primary enquiry form spans six broad solution families and dozens of checkboxes. That may reduce completed, well-qualified cyber enquiries.

## Competitor patterns

1. **Netitude:** uses a dedicated audit landing page with a named output and process ([source](https://www.netitude.co.uk/it-audit)).
2. **Secure Chain:** uses a short posture-assessment form asking what prompted the conversation ([source](https://securechaingroup.com/)).
3. **Morcan:** separates a free security review from a migration assessment and states what the review covers ([source](https://www.morcan.co.uk/)).

## One hypothesis

A cyber-only audit CTA and short form will earn more qualified cybersecurity conversations than routing cyber visitors into the full multi-service contact form.

## One proposed experiment

On the cybersecurity page, test “Request a 20-minute Cyber Risk Triage” against the existing contact route. Ask only work email, company size, current trigger, and desired outcome; show the audit case-study proof beside the CTA. Measure qualified conversations per CTA click, not raw submissions.
