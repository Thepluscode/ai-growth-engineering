# Growth Leak Teardown — Probrand

Research date: 2026-08-19

## ICP fit
- **Observed:** UK IT-services and cybersecurity provider supporting organisations with roughly 25–500 users.
- **Inferred:** Strong fit.

## Current offer
- **Observed:** A free Cyber Security Risk Assessment completed through a guided calendar session or self-serve link, returning a score, priority risks and action plan.

## Primary CTA
- **Observed:** “Book an Assessment Date” and “Get Your Self-Serve Assessment Link.”

## Acquisition surfaces
- **Observed:** Assessment campaign, downloadable guide, marketplace, IT-service pages, phone, assessment email and forms.

## Observable funnel
- **Observed:** Cyber-risk page → guided calendar or self-serve request → report → optional remediation support.
- **Unknown:** Buyer self-selection, mode-specific completion, report review and accepted-opportunity conversion.

## Proof
- **Observed:** 30+ years, defined 25–500-user fit, a detailed output, recognised standards and a dedicated `cra@probrand.co.uk` route.

## Likely commercial leak
- **Inference, not diagnosis:** The two modes are valuable but the page does not visibly state which buyer should choose each, potentially adding avoidable choice at the start.

## Competitor patterns
1. **CyberLab:** makes expert guidance part of one three-step assessment ([source](https://cyberlab.co.uk/services/posture-assessment/)).
2. **Xperience:** uses a single self-serve assessment under ten minutes ([source](https://www.xperience-group.com/solutions/cyber-security/cyber-security-overview/)).
3. **OryxAlign:** distinguishes a quick score from an in-depth maturity assessment ([source](https://www.oryxalign.com/cyber)).

## One hypothesis
Suitability copy—“choose guided if…” versus “choose self-serve if…”—will increase starts and improve mode-fit.

## One proposed experiment
Add one qualification sentence under each mode and retain mode in CRM. Compare started assessments, completed reports and sales-accepted follow-ups by mode.
