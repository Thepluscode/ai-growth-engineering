# `EXP-ACQ-0001` — Batch 04 Qualification

Date qualified: 2026-08-19

Purpose: create a fourth controlled 10-account research batch while the first 19 confirmed sends
gather replies. Qualification is not a diagnosis, send authorisation, or evidence that a leak exists.

## Qualification rule

An account qualifies when current public evidence shows a UK cybersecurity consultancy, MSP or MSSP
with a commercially meaningful service, an observable acquisition route, credible delivery proof,
and a plausible commercial owner. Observation and research hypotheses remain separate.

## 1. Central Technology

- **Observed fit:** UK managed IT, cloud, communications and cyber provider with a detailed Cyber
  Security Review and managed-security packages.
- **Observed route/proof:** the review covers infrastructure, M365, policies and scanning, then
  produces residual-risk findings in a workshop; public customer evidence and certifications are
  visible; `sales@ct.uk` is published.
- **Buyer route:** route through the published sales inbox to the cyber commercial owner.
- **Research hypothesis:** determine whether “Apply now” creates more perceived commitment than the
  review actually requires.
- **Sources:** [review](https://www.ct.co.uk/services/cyber-security-review/), [contact](https://www.ct.co.uk/contact-us/)
