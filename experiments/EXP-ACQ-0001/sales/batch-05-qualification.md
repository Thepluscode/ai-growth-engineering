## Qualification rule

Use the same current-evidence, UK-market, observable-route and credible-proof rule as Batch 04.

## 1. M247

- **Observed fit:** UK connectivity, cloud and managed-cyber provider serving growing organisations.
- **Observed route/proof:** a free M365 Security Assessment promises a posture score, benchmark and
  recommendations; its wider managed-security offer includes health checks, hardening, XDR and 24/7
  response; `sales@m247.com` is public.
- **Buyer route:** use the published sales inbox and request the managed-cyber commercial owner.
- **Research hypothesis:** determine whether the free M365 assessment remains the visible first step
  from the broader managed-security journey.
- **Sources:** [M365 assessment](https://info.m247.com/m365-security-assessment), [managed cyber](https://m247.com/services/managed-cyber-security/)

## 2. Atlas Cloud

- **Observed fit:** independent UK managed IT and cyber provider focused on professional-services and
  other security-conscious organisations.
- **Observed route/proof:** its 12-point Cyber Security MOT has a certificate, health report and expert
  review, while deeper reconnaissance and penetration testing are separately defined; thousands of
  audits, ISO evidence and `hello@atlascloud.co.uk` are public.
- **Buyer route:** route to the cyber commercial owner through the published company inbox.
- **Research hypothesis:** determine whether buyers understand when to start with MOT Step 1, Step 2
  or the broader reconnaissance route.
- **Sources:** [Cyber Security MOT](https://atlascloud.co.uk/it-services/cyber-security-consultancy/mot/), [consultancy ladder](https://atlascloud.co.uk/it-services/cyber-security-consultancy/), [about](https://atlascloud.co.uk/about-us/)
