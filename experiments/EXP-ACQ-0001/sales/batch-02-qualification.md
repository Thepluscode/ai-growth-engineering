# `EXP-ACQ-0001` — Batch 02 Qualification

Date qualified: 2026-08-19

Purpose: preserve a second 10-account research batch while Batch 01 gathers replies. Qualification is
not a diagnosis, teardown, send authorisation, or claim that a funnel problem exists.

## Qualification rule

An account qualifies when current public evidence shows a UK cybersecurity consultancy, MSP or MSSP
with a commercially meaningful service, an observable acquisition route, credible delivery proof,
and a plausible founder, commercial or marketing buyer. Observations and research hypotheses remain
separate.
